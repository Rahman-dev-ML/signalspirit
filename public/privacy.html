Signal Sprint Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026. Developer: Lunox.

This mobile release works offline. It does not include accounts, advertisements, in-app purchases, analytics, third-party game publisher services, or tracking.

Game progress, scores and preferences are saved on your device using browser storage and native app preferences. They are not sent to Lunox. Operating-system backup settings may include app preferences in your device backup.

The app does not request location, camera, contacts or microphone access. Optional haptic feedback is handled by your device.

You can delete saved progress using Settings → Reset Progress. Uninstalling or clearing app data can also remove it.

Apple, Google and your device may independently process store, purchase or diagnostic information under their own policies. This policy describes the game itself.
